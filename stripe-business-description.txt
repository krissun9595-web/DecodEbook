DecodEbook is an AI-powered reading platform that transforms uploaded documents (PDF, EPUB, TXT) into interactive, multi-modal learning experiences. Features include AI voice synthesis for audiobook playback, AI-generated podcasts with multiple host styles, sentence-level bilingual translation for language learners, concept visualization, video chapter summaries, and an AI reading assistant chatbot. The platform offers a free tier with limited usage and paid subscription plans (monthly and annual) for extended access to AI generation features.
