Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-18

This Privacy Policy explains how DecodEbook (operated by the DecodEbook team; "we", "us") handles your information when you use the DecodEbook application and decodebook.app (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. What We Collect

  • Account: your email and authentication IDs; if you use third-party sign-in (Google, GitHub, X, Discord), basic profile info from that provider.
  • Content: files you upload (PDF, EPUB, text), your notes, and the materials the Service generates from them.
  • Usage & logs: actions taken, credits and models used, and technical data such as IP address, device/browser, and timestamps.
  • Payment: processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers, only billing metadata (e.g., subscription status, customer/transaction IDs).
  • Referral: referral codes, clicks/sign-ups, and related bonus-credit activity.
  • Local storage: your browser caches your library, settings, and some generated media on your device.

2. How We Use It

To provide and operate the Service and the features you request; to process your content through AI providers; to manage accounts, credits, subscriptions, payments, and referrals; to secure the Service and prevent abuse; to improve the Service (using aggregated or operational data); to communicate with you; and to comply with the law.

3. AI & Third-Party Providers

To deliver the features you request, the relevant parts of your content are sent to AI providers and returned to you — currently Google (Gemini), OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, BytePlus, fal.ai, and ElevenLabs. We also rely on Supabase (authentication, database, storage), Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), Stripe (payments), and, if you choose, Google/GitHub/X/Discord for sign-in. These providers process data under their own terms.

Training: we do not use your content to train our own models. Whether an AI provider retains or uses submitted data (including to train its own models) is governed by that provider's API terms.

4. Storage, Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored with Supabase and delivered via Cloudflare; some generated media is stored only on your device. We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to meet legal or dispute obligations. You can delete your content or your entire account at any time from within the app — account deletion permanently removes your account and associated data.

5. How We Share

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, to comply with law or lawful requests, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

6. Your Rights

Under the PRC Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), you may access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of your personal information, withdraw consent, and object to certain processing. Use in-app account deletion, or contact support@decodebook.app. If you are outside Mainland China, you may have additional rights under your local laws (e.g., GDPR or CCPA) — contact us to exercise them.

7. Security

We use measures such as encryption in transit and access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information; if you are under 18, use the Service only with a guardian's consent. If you believe a child under 14 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including by the providers listed above.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide reasonable notice; the effective date reflects the latest revision.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@decodebook.app.
