## PART I — STRATEGY (English)

### 1. Product in one line
DecodEbook turns any PDF or EPUB into a faithful **bilingual reading room** — original and translation side by side — with read-aloud, instant definitions, an AI tutor that actually read your book, auto-podcasts, visual summaries, a notebook, and cross-device sync.

### 2. Who we talk to (3 audiences)
- **Language learners** — want comprehensible input: bilingual alignment, tap-to-define, read-aloud with sentence highlighting, a vocabulary notebook.
- **Students / researchers** — want to survive dense PDFs: faithful figures/footnotes/cross-references, mind maps, an AI tutor grounded in the source, chapter summaries & podcasts.
- **Re-readers / book lovers** — want depth and immersion: faithful typography, EPUB fidelity, listen-while-reading, notes that make a re-read richer.

### 3. Channels (self-media, bilingual)
- **Global/EN:** YouTube (Shorts + long-form), TikTok, Instagram Reels, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit (r/languagelearning, r/GradSchool, r/books), Substack/Medium.
- **China/中文:** 抖音 (Douyin), 小红书 (RED), Bilibili (B站), 视频号, 微信公众号 (WeChat), 知乎 (Zhihu).
- **One shoot → many cuts:** every long piece is sliced into 3–6 vertical shorts + 1 text post. Caption in both languages when effort allows.

### 4. The three content pillars (= the three formats)
1. **Founder IP** — the maker's voice: why this exists, how it's built, what reading should be.
2. **Virtual IP "Deco" scenario shorts** — a character-driven 30s–60s story per feature, generated via Seedance from prompts.
3. **Formal feature-launch videos** — clean, polished product explainers.

### 5. Cadence & funnel (suggested)
- Weekly: 2× Deco shorts (top-of-funnel, shareable) + 1× Founder piece (trust) + 1× text post.
- Monthly: 1× Formal feature video (conversion) + 1 "build-in-public" recap.
- Funnel: Deco shorts **attract** → Founder content **builds trust** → Feature videos **convert** → free credits CTA → Pro.

### 6. Hooks & CTA library (reusable)
- Hooks: "Reading a book in a language you don't fully know…"; "Every PDF paper is fighting you. Here's the fix."; "I re-read this book 5 times. This time was different."
- CTAs: "Drop your hardest PDF in the link." · "Try it free — first book's on me." · "Comment a book, I'll decode a page live."
- Primary metric per pillar: Deco = reach/shares; Founder = follows/comments; Feature = click-through → sign-up → activated (first generation).

## 第一部分 — 策略（中文）

### 1. 一句话介绍产品
DecodEbook 把任意 PDF / EPUB 变成一间「忠实的双语阅读室」——原文与译文并排对照——并配有朗读、即点即译、真正「读过你这本书」的 AI 导师、自动播客、可视化摘要、笔记本与跨设备同步。

### 2. 面向三类人群
- **语言学习者** —— 需要可理解输入：双语对齐、点词即译、带句子高亮的朗读、生词本。
- **学生 / 研究者** —— 需要啃下硬核 PDF：忠实还原图表 / 脚注 / 交叉引用、思维导图、基于原文的 AI 导师、章节摘要与播客。
- **重读者 / 爱书人** —— 需要深度与沉浸：忠实排版、EPUB 还原、边听边读、让重读更丰富的笔记。

### 3. 渠道（自媒体，双语）
- **海外 / 英文：** YouTube（Shorts + 长视频）、TikTok、Instagram Reels、X、LinkedIn、Reddit、Substack / Medium。
- **国内 / 中文：** 抖音、小红书、B 站、视频号、微信公众号、知乎。
- **一次拍摄 → 多次切条：** 每条长内容切成 3–6 条竖版短视频 + 1 条图文，尽量中英双语字幕。

### 4. 三大内容支柱（= 三种形式）
1. **开发者个人 IP** —— 作者的声音：为何而做、如何打磨、阅读本该是什么样。
2. **虚拟 IP「Deco」情景短视频** —— 以角色驱动、由提示词经 Seedance 生成的 30–60 秒每功能一个小故事。
3. **正式功能发布视频** —— 干净、精致的产品讲解。

### 5. 节奏与漏斗（建议）
- 每周：2 条 Deco 短视频（引流、易传播）+ 1 条开发者内容（建立信任）+ 1 条图文。
- 每月：1 条正式功能视频（转化）+ 1 条「公开构建」复盘。
- 漏斗：Deco 短视频**吸引** → 开发者内容**建信任** → 功能视频**转化** → 免费额度 CTA → Pro 订阅。

### 6. 钩子与 CTA 素材库（可复用）
- 钩子：「读一本你还没完全掌握语言的书……」；「每篇 PDF 论文都在跟你作对，这是解法。」；「这本书我读了 5 遍，这一次不一样。」
- CTA：「把你最难的 PDF 丢到链接里。」·「免费试试，第一本书我请你。」·「评论区留一本书，我直播解码一页。」
- 各支柱核心指标：Deco = 触达 / 转发；开发者 = 关注 / 评论；功能 = 点击 → 注册 → 激活（首次生成）。

## Appendix — Production notes / 制作备注
- **Brand consistency / 品牌统一:** cyan accent, calm confident tone, "fidelity + understanding" as the north star. 青色点缀、沉稳自信、以「忠实 + 理解」为核心。
